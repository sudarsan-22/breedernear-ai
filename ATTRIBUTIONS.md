# Attributions

Every image, dataset, font or other asset in this repository that we did not create ourselves must be listed here **before it is committed**, with its source and licence. Assets without a clear licence that permits use must not be added.

Policy:
- Product brands, breeders and breeder names are **fictional**. No real brand names, logos, product photos or real breeders.
- Product and listing images are generated by us with a Google Gemini image model, or are our own photos. The model is recorded below once used.
- Animal photos are our own (or taken with the animal owner's permission), or CC0 / public-domain images listed below.
- The sample "scam post" screenshots are written and made by us. Registration numbers in the simulated registry use a `SIM-` prefix and are fictional.
- Nothing is copied from the earlier PetZonic platform repositories (code, designs, images, data or brand files).

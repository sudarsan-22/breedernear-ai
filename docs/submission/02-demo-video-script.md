# Demo Video Script

**Hard limit:** strictly under 3:00 (T&C: Deliverables). **Target:** 2:40. **Never exceed 2:55.**
**Format:** screen recording of the **live deployed URL** (show the address bar), English voice-over by the founder, captions on, 1080p.
**Upload:** YouTube, Unlisted or Public. Test the link in incognito while logged out.

Record breeder scenes on a phone (or a phone-sized browser window) and buyer scenes on a laptop. Use the demo identities and the sample assets in `data/samples/`, which are proven to work.

| Time | Scene | On screen | Voice-over (draft) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | **Founder hook** | Founder on camera or a title card: "The breeder is 50 metres away." | "For five years I bred and sold birds in Coimbatore. Every week I watched new customers walk into a shop and overpay, while a breeder on the same street sold healthier birds for less. They just couldn't find each other." |
| 0:20–0:30 | **Intro** | PetZonic AI home, URL visible | "PetZonic AI connects breeders and buyers directly and safely, using Gemini agents on Google Cloud. Retail and Commerce theme." |
| 0:30–1:00 | **Breeder copilot** | Phone: "Try as Karthik" → 2 lovebird photos + mixed Tamil-English message → draft card with fair-price bar → Publish → ✅ TRUSTED, reasons | "Breeders post like they do on WhatsApp: photos and a quick message, even in Tamil. Gemini turns it into a complete listing and suggests a fair price. One tap to publish, and every listing is screened first." |
| 1:00–1:20 | **Blocked** | Message offering "pachai kili" (a native parakeet) → ⛔ BLOCKED with explanation | "Protected Indian birds are illegal to trade. PetZonic blocks them in code, so no prompt can talk it into publishing one." |
| 1:20–1:50 | **Buyer match** | Laptop: "Try as Priya" → "pet bird for my 8-year-old, flat in Tiruppur, ₹3000" → species options → listing cards with trust badges, distance and fair-price bar | "Buyers just describe their life. The matching agent suggests suitable pets, then finds trusted breeders nearby. Each card shows the trust level and whether the price is fair." |
| 1:50–2:10 | **Starter kit** | "I'll take the budgie pair" → kit cards with total → care plan with "see a vet if" | "Then a care agent builds the right starter kit, with a cage big enough for a pair, plus a two-week care plan." |
| 2:10–2:25 | **Is this listing safe?** | Upload the sample WhatsApp-style scam screenshot → ⚠️ CAUTION: price 70% below range, "full advance, courier only", questions to ask | "And for posts seen elsewhere, buyers can check them first. PetZonic spots the warning signs before anyone pays." |
| 2:25–2:40 | **Architecture and close** | Architecture diagram → activity panel → logo + URL | "Five ADK agents on Cloud Run with Gemini, Firestore and Cloud Storage, tested with evals. PetZonic AI: find the breeder next door, and trust what you buy." |

## Recording checklist

- [ ] App warmed up (open it once before recording)
- [ ] Clean guest session; notifications off; bookmarks bar hidden; readable zoom
- [ ] Quiet room; voice-over synced
- [ ] Captions added and corrected
- [ ] No real personal data, real brands, real breeders' names, or others' photos visible
- [ ] Every feature shown is live and working
- [ ] Duration checked on the **uploaded** video: ≤ 2:55
- [ ] Link tested in incognito while logged out

## Eligibility (T&C: Team Composition, Eligibility)

- 2–4 members per team. You can't participate solo.
- Every member is **21+**, a **working professional, entrepreneur, or startup founder**, and **physically based in and a legal resident of a JAPAC country**.
- **No students, full-time or part-time.** One student disqualifies the **entire team**, even if discovered after shortlisting or after the finale.
- The organiser may verify government ID, employment and age **at any time**. Keep proof of employment ready.
- One person = one team = one submission under one theme. Cross-team registration disqualifies **all** associated entries.

## The six themes

1. BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences
2. **Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences** ← BreederNear
3. Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency
4. Media, Content & Digital Experiences
5. Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity
6. Sustainability & Social Impact

There is **no healthcare theme**. BreederNear is a pet-care **commerce** experience, and the Retail & Commerce theme explicitly lists *product discovery, personalisation, conversational shopping, customer insights and fraud prevention*. See [../product/01-problem-and-vision.md](../product/01-problem-and-vision.md).

## Mandatory technology

- **Gemini or Gemma models**, or an agentic platform (Agent Platform, Antigravity, AI Studio). We use Gemini + ADK.
- **Deployed on Google Cloud**, via Cloud Run or Firebase. We use Cloud Run.
- Built **primarily** on Google Cloud. Solutions built primarily on another cloud are disqualified.

## Mandatory deliverables (T&C: Deliverables)

1. Working, deployed **live URL** (Cloud Run / GCP / Firebase)
2. **Demo video strictly under 3 minutes**, at a public link
3. **Public GitHub repository** with the source code
4. **Presentation deck** (PDF) covering the **solution architecture and the business case**

Incomplete submissions are ineligible. Details are in [04-submission-checklist.md](04-submission-checklist.md).
